# Washington D.C. 2010

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0557.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0557_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>It had been a few years, and my parents wanted to take my nephews to D.C., so I tagged along.  Upon arriving, we boarded on of the double decker buses for a tour around the city.  The tour, which started at Union Station, made it's first stop at the U.S. Capital. Thankfully most of the construction that I remember from previous trips has been completed, so this time I was actually able to "see" the capital.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0561.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0561_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>The view from the top of the bus was actually pretty good, albeit, fairly cold.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0603.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0603_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>
Continuing on, we head up the hill to visit the cathedral.  We didn't stop to get out, but the bus did pause for a moment or two.  Just long enough to take a few pictures.  One item of note.  When they say "stay seated", they mean stay seated.  The power lines and tree branches that cross the street are literally just above the height of the bus.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0604.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0604_SM.JPG" alt="" width="149" height="200" /></a>The next day, we start out at the Washington Monument.  I have to say that the new way of getting people up to the top is much better than it had been.  Last I remember, the line wrapped around the monument several times, and many people wasted a great number of hours.  Now you make a reservation and show up for your allotted time slot.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0617.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0617_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>I decided to take a few pictures while at the top.  The view was actually quite good and the weather was extremely cooperative.  Looking down on the Lincoln Memorial, the view now includes the World War II Memorial.  I'd been to it in years past, but the view from the air definitely puts it into perspective.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0623.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0623_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>As you can see from the shot on the left, the White House is in the process of setting up their Christmas tree.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0631.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0631_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Going to the east side, I managed to get a fairly good shot of the Mall, Smithsonian Museums, and the Capital.  For some reason I keep thinking the distance is a lot less than it actually is.  Maybe this picture will remind me ;-)
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0662.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0662_SM.JPG" alt="" width="149" height="200" /></a>Sunday night, my sister wanted me to go to one of her favorite hangouts.  First we stopped by her place to fix a few things.  Then we took the short walk over to The Mighty Pint.  On the way, we meet up with a friend of hers, Roxana.  Cute, are they not? :-)
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0675.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0675_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Monday morning, we head over to the Library of Congress for a few minutes so we don't have to wait outside at the capital too long (in the cold).  Come to find out, the capital has spent a few dollars, about a billion, re-doing the way visitors get into the building.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0698.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0698_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Inside, not much has actually changed.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0718.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0718_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>The two of my nephews who were able to get out that day are featured on the right.  For the most part, they were fairly well behaved.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0726.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0726_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Now we come to one of my favorite parts of the city, at least favorite amongst the touristy parts of the city, the Air and Space Museum.  This is my first time there since Space Ship One has been added to the aircraft of display.  It is hard to believe that a ship so small managed to accomplish so much.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0802.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0802_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>At the other end of the accomplishment spectrum is the Wright Flyer.  According to the signs in the museum, this is the original 1903 craft.  Other than the cloth covering the wings, everything is original.  I'm just impressed that it flew for as long as it did, when it did.  I'm also glad it did ;-)
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0814.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0814_SM.JPG" alt="" width="149" height="200" /></a>Ethan is apparently in a penguin phase.  Here he is with his alter-ego.  How Ethan got in the case and managed to get the penguin to kneel next to him, I'll never know :-o  Seriously though, both he and David seemed to enjoy the natural history museum, especially the dinosaurs!
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0820.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0820_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>T-Rex and the brontosaurus (at least I think that is a brontosaurus) haven't changed much in the 20 or so years that I've been going to D.C.  This time around I noticed a few more creatures than I have in the past.  Some that I hadn't ever heard of before, like an "even".  When Kate pointed out the name, all I could say was, "that's odd"!
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0829.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0829_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a> <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0910.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0910_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Taking off from KDCA, the weather was just good enough to allow a couple pictures before we were up in the clouds.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0912.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/DC_11_2010/IMG_0912_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>I wonder how long it will be before I'm back?
