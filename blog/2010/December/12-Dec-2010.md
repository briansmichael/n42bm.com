# Rudder

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5.0
Manual Ref: Page 7-2
Partner: N/A</span></em>

The rudder went together very quickly.  There aren't that many pieces.  I nearly had to make a call to Vans as I couldn't figure out that R-1006 was made from VA-140.  Going over the plans again (actually a few times), I saw that they say to make R-1006 out of VA-140.  Moving forward, everything cleco'ed together much nicer than the vertical stabilizer.  Now time to final drill and dimple!  For a time-lapse video of the assembly process, see <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/2010/12_12_2010/RV-10-Rudder_1.mov">rudder assembly</a>
