After 2 years of waiting, it is finally here!  The kits was shipped via ABF (Aurora, OR -&gt; Cheyenne, WY -&gt; Omaha, 
NE -&gt; Little Rock, AR -&gt; Fulton, MS -&gt; Atlanta, GA) and made it to my house around 11 AM today.  Spent a good 
portion of the day inventory'ing more parts than I thought would fit into the box.  I'm already impressed with the 
engineering Vans has put into just the shipping of the parts!
