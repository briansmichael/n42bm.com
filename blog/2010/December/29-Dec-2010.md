The temperature got to a balmy 53 Fahrenheit today!  So, I'm continuing from where I left off...
<ol>
	<li>Mix a few ladles of Akzo primer part A with part B in a plastic paint bucket, and get HPLV gun ready to spray.</li>
	<li>Put on a full body paint suit (you're going to need it!)</li>
	<li>Spray each part enough to completely cover with primer, but not so much that it runs.</li>
	<li>Allow parts to dry for about an hour, even though they are probably dry in about 10 to 15 minutes.</li>
</ol>
The Akzo primer actually does go on pretty nice, and I now understand why in all of my research I could not find any pictures or videos of the priming process.  It is a VERY messy process.  Even with a fairly elaborate paint booth, a little bit of the over spray managed to get out, but not enough to harm anything.  Since I had not managed to clean the rudder skins before I started spraying primer, I still have to do those pieces, but that should be a small task.  You can see the primer "dust" on the vinyl covering that is still on the vertical stabilizer.  It truly gets on everything.
