At long last, I've driven my first rivet!  I knew this was going to be a long and involved project, but just the vertical stabilizer took a huge amount of time to rivet together.  Good thing I like a challenge :-).  I marked the first driven rivet head in black with a sharpie.  I have no idea if the mark will stay there, but it'll be neat to look back on when the whole thing is done.  Here are some pictures of the progress.
