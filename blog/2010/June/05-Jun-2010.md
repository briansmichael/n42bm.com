# Saranac Lake

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0896.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0896_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Jonathan and I go on a camping trip about every other year.  This year we were trying to go to Canada, but the logistics were just too much.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0904.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0904_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>He came up with the idea of Saranac Lake, and it turned out to be a good one.  We got his truck all packed up, then headed over to his church for a quick softball game.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0906.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0906_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Our first night was in Lee, MA, which was just a few hours down the road.  Since the church is actually east of Worcester, it took a little longer to get to Lee than it would have if we'd left directly from Jonathan's house, but it was a game he wasn't willing to miss.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0937.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0937_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Once we got to the lakes, we "put in" on Lower Saranac Lake.  Between the Lower Saranac and Middle Saranac there is a lock.  This is the first time I've ever been through a lock, much less a self service one.  On the right is the view from the lower lake.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0939.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0939_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Naturally, while we were there, we had to get out and look around.  The instructions for operating the lock were pretty straight forward.  We also we lucky in that a ranger was there, and he actually did most of the work for us.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0940.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0940_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>I made sure to get several shots of the whole thing.  It is really quite elegant in it's simplicity.  Gravity runs the thing.  The doors on the lower side (right) are held closed by the pressure from the water of the middle lake.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0941.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0941_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>At least when the doors from the middle lake are open.  As long as one set of doors are closed at all times, all is well.  There is a overrun not too far off, so the lake level shouldn't get so high as to over take the locks.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0949.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0949_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Once we got to our camping spot we did a little setting up, found a perch up on some rocks, sat back, and watched the sun set and moon rise.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0954.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0954_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Our camp site was on an island, so we weren't too concerned about bears.  There were, however, a few squirrels, so we did have to keep our stuff somewhat secured.  Jonathan and I did the REI freeze dried approach to food.  I think we'll try more of a mix of things in the future.  And let me just say that the freeze dried biscuits and gravy... well, I don't think we'll be doing that one again ;-).
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0968.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Saranac_Lake_05_2010/Brian_Saranac_Lake/IMG_0968_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>The trail leading up to the camp site came down to the area seen on the right.  Getting out of a canoe here is not exactly easy, but it was doable.  The rocks did provide a convenient location to rest the canoe.
