# Another “Class Project” update

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0874.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0874_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>It has been awhile.  Work and other things get in the way.  That's something I'm going to have to figure out when it comes to doing the real thing.  I'll never finish the plane if I go at this rate.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0875.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0875_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>At this point I've riveted a large number of the AN426AD3-3 rivets that hold the skin on to the spar and ribs.  Since I'm not going to prime this project, it is fairly difficult to get a picture of the inside, but here are a couple attempts.  To the right you should be able to see where the bucking bar slipped a little while driving the rivet with my 3x gun.  I'm still new to this, and all I can do is hope I get a lot better.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0876.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0876_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Hopefully at least one of these is good enough to be able to see how things look on the inside.  The outside is definitely easier to get a picture of.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0877.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0877_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>Looking at the outside, you can see the left is cleco'd and the right is mostly riveted.  The instructions (under the workpiece) state that the rivets near the top of the workpiece will require either a special bucking bar, or a special squeezer yoke.  Personally, I'm leaning towards the special yoke.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0878.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0878_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a> Just for the sake of doing it, I took another shot from the other side.  Going forward, I may not take as many pictures... but we'll see.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0879.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/07_18_2010/IMG_0879_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>On the right, I've removed the clecos and let the skin open up a little.  It is a bit easier to see down the inside, but it is still pretty shinny down in there.
