# Gulf Shores

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0001.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0001_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>The Thursday night "ground school" gang (including myself) wanted to go on a trip, now that we are all licensed private pilots.  Dave has his own plane, which left Mike and I to rent one from The Flight School.  We ended up taking N7262X.  Come to find out, the power connector in 62X is a special connector, not the typical cigarette lighter.  So our plans of using Mike's aera were dashed.  We did get to use it a little, as it had at least an hour's worth of battery.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0002.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0002_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>And it is a good thing we had it.  The weather wasn't completely perfect.  Thunderstorms in the area around Gulf Shores due to a tropical storm that was nearby.  However, most of the flight was pretty nice weather wise.  Mike wanted to see how high we could get.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0003.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0003_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>On the ground at KLZU, the reported weather was 3,500 feet density altitude, so I wasn't expecting to get too high.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0008.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0008_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>By the time we got south of Atlanta, we were coming up on 10,500 feet.  At this point, we could have spun the plane and had lots of time to recover.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0016.JPG"><img class="alignright" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0016_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>As proof of the altitude we got to, I made sure to snap a picture of the panel showing us at nearly full throttle and just a little above 10,500.  The clouds made the trip a little more eventful, as we had to do a few left and right turns to avoid them.  We're not yet instrument rated, you know.
<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0034.JPG"><img class="alignleft" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0034_SM.JPG" alt="" width="200" height="150" /></a>Before landing at KJKA in Gulf Shores, I took a picture of the two of us with my iPhone.  Would you go on a trip with these two?

When it came time to land, I took some video of the whole thing.  It is about 15 minutes long.  You can somewhat see the stormy weather to the right and the shore to the left.  Pensacola approach was vectoring us a little to avoid the weather and their airspace.  I think we timed things just right.  <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/GulfShores2010/IMG_0041.mov">IMG_0041.mov</a>
