# A Bumpy Ride

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/1/10_A_Bumpy_Ride_files/IMG_0434.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object503.png" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>Today I had the pleasure to fly with Ernie Schnaak, one of the chief instructors at the <a href="http://www.theflightschool.com/">Flight School</a>.  He has more experience than I doubt I’ll ever have, and was a blast to fly with.  Today’s <a href="../Pictures/Pages/Solo_Cross-Country_Progress_Check.html">lesson</a> was a progress check after my solo cross-country.  I must have done well, As my log book reads “XC Progress checks completed ‘very good job’”.  Now I just need to find the time (and money) to do my long solo cross-country.  The discussion after the lesson was about who I want to take my check ride with.  I guess I’m getting close to being a licensed pilot!
