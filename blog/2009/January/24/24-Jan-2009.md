# Trivia, but different

Quite the change of pace from our usual Pizza Cafe trivia night on Mondays.  Summit’s is significantly more noisy, but the questions are on par in difficulty.  Naturally, we didn’t win.  Perhaps because it was from 10 PM ‘till midnight.
