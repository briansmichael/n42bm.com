# Almost In The Air

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/2/25_Almost_In_The_Air_files/55F.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object001_6.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>I came about as close as you can get to flying today.  I got to the flight school, reviewed my flight plan with Sean (the instructor) got in the plane, taxied to the runway, got clearance to takeoff.... then I hear “abort your takeoff” from ATC.  The weather had deteriorated to wear I wasn’t allowed to fly, and my instructor called the tower to have them stop me.  Oh well.  I’ve re-scheduled for Monday.  Let’s hope for better weather!
