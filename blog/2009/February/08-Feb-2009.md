# It’s not flying, but...

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/2/7_Its_not_flying,_but..._files/cover_65.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object337.png" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:50px; height:46px;"/></a>I finally made it to an EAA 690 Saturday Pancake Breakfast!  I’m still amazed at how nice everyone is.  The pilot community is terrific, and I wish I had done this YEARS ago.  Being the new guy, I took the guys in the tower some pancakes, and got to look around a little bit.  It’s good to know what the guys on the other end of the radio see and do.  Afterwards it was Pinewood Derby day for the nephews cub scout pack.  Ethan got to spend the day in the hospital with an upset stomach, but is doing ok now.  All-in-all, it was a rather eventful day.
