# In the Air

Pancake Breakfast with my parents.  They suffered through chatting with all the pilot types there, but I think they enjoyed the meal.  I stuck around for the presentation on LSAs.  I was scheduled for my long solo x-country at noon, but fog pretty much prevented my departure.  Then trying to plan the trip around weather in the area just proved to be too much.  I opted for a local solo, as I need the solo time in the log book, and I need to get in the air as much as possible.  The x-country will happen...  don’t know when, but it will happen!
