# The Flight School Open House

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/3/28_The_Flight_School_Open_House_files/IMG_0483.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object001_3.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>It was raining, and little windy, but a lot of <a href="../Pictures/Pages/Flight_School_Open_House.html">people</a> managed to turn out for The Flight School’s Open House.  Cessna decided to keep their new planes hangared at KPDK because hail was predicted.  Why ruin a multi-million dollar plane with hail damage?  At any rate, they’ll be having another event probably in early May when hopefully the weather will be nicer.  I think everyone had a good time, and having the space shuttle land was a great close to the party.
