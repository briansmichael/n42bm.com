# The Long Solo Cross Country

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/3/10_The_Long_Solo_Cross_Country_files/62X.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object000_1.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>I finally did it!  The weather was still cutting it a little close with low clouds and windy conditions, but it finally was within my minimums, so I went.  KLZU (Gwinnett County, GA) to KGMU (Greenville Dowtown, SC), to KWDR (Winder, GA) back to KLZU.  All told, 2.6 hours added to the log book, and the first lesson completed in over 2 months!  The next is a local solo, so hopefully Sunday I’ll be able to knock out another one. :-)
