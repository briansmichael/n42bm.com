# Quality Time at the Airport

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/4/4_Quality_Time_at_the_Airport_files/62X.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object000_1.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>Today was a great day for flying.  After nearly 4 months of rainy weather, the skies cleared and the wind disappeared.  I got to the airport a little before 8 AM for the EAA 690 Pancake Breakfast (my parent’s also joined me for that).  The flight school instructors didn’t have any students, so they also came over for some food, and brought a friend, Conrad, from back home (Norway).  From what I gather, Conrad was getting the “American Experience”.  They had been to a Gladiators hockey game and all of the highlights Atlanta has to offer downtown.  After breakfast we headed over to the flight school to start the lesson.  This time we added a twist, Conrad rode back seat during the lesson.  Now I’m still a student, practicing for my checkride, thus performing stalls, steep turns, simulated IR, unusual attitude recovery, etc.  Conrad is not a pilot, and had been in a Cessna once before, probably 15 years ago.  What an experience he must have had.  The lesson went well. I have some work to do regarding checklists and emergency briefings, but flew the airplane within PTS for nearly the entire flight.  Having the extra weight in the back is certainly something I’m glad I’ve experienced before trying that on my own.  The plane just feels different.  Next up, another local solo on Tuesday, if the weather holds.
