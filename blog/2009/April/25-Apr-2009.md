# A Perfect Day

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/4/25_Entry_1_files/76C.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object004_1.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:51px; height:38px;"/></a>I finally managed to convince my girlfriend to come visit me.  She has a friend who’s B’day is tomorrow, so she brought her along for company...  it turns out to be a great idea.  They arrived around lunch, then we did some shopping downtown.  Afterwards, we went flying in N1576C.  I had never flown a G1000 172R before.  It is very different.  The girls hadn’t ever been flying with me, so it was a first for them as well.  I think Charlotte has some experience in small planes, but I’m not sure Tina (her friend) did.  I know they both took a lot of pictures, and hopefully they’ll send me some of them so I can post them here.  Then to cap off the evening, we all went to see the Stone Mountain Laser Show.  I don’t either of them had seen it.  A fairly packed day that I would not have traded for anything!
