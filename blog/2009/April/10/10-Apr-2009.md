# A Dark, Stormy Night

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Entries/2009/4/10_Entry_1_files/cover_65.jpg"><img src="http://www.brianmichael.org/web/Blog/Media/object001_3.jpg" style="float:left; padding-right:10px; padding-bottom:10px; width:50px; height:46px;"/></a>After a rather uneventful day at the office, I met up with the usual crowd for the EAA 690 meeting.  The weather was looking a little rough as we were eating at the <a href="http://theflyingmachine.com/">Flying Machine</a>, but it was all talk and no action.  After dinner, however, the action started.  The radio was reporting tornado warnings for the area.  We never saw one, but there was plenty of hail.  Never a good thing on an airport!
