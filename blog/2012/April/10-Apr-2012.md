# Riveting the left outboard top skin

<em>Hours: 5.0
Manual Ref: Page 16-3 step 1, 2, and 3
Partner: n/a</em>

Riveting the outboard top left skin went pretty much like the inboard skin.  Lots of lifting the wing vertically then horizontal to place rivets in the plethora of holes.  A few rivets had to be drilled out due to not having the wing placed exactly right, but the vast majority went in without an issue.
