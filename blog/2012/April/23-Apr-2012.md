# Septic tank

After a bit of diagnosing, it turns out the real problem was roots in the septic tank. The story goes as follows:

After spending a few hours pulling up carpet and throwing soaked padding into the backyard, a friend came over to help try to figure out what was wrong. The result was a call to Roto-Rooter. The Roto-Rooter guy came out, took a quick look at the problem and asked "when was the last time you had your septic tank pumped?". It was eerie that some neighbors of mine had been talking recently about having their septic tank pumped. In the interest of not paying $450 to Roto-Rooter to _maybe_ fix the problem, I contacted Aames Plumbing to have the tank pumped. The guy from Aames (Nick), opened up the tank and pretty quickly determined the problem was roots. So my $300 tank pumping turned into a $1300 pumping and de-rooting project.

Thankfully, all is now well with the septic system... now on to fixing the house!
