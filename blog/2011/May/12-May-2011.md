# Trim Tabs

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5
Manual Ref: Page 9-16; Page 9-17 steps 1 through 7
Partner: N/A</span></em>

Today was trim tab day.  Making the homemade break was quick and easy, and does a very effective job of making sure the bend in the trim tab skin is even.  Bending the ends was another story.  The plans say to place the wood block 1/32" from the edge of the skin.  Even with the double sided tape, my wood block moved upon hitting it with the flush set.  Eventually, I got it all straightened out, but it was a lot of work. The 15 degree bend in the lower skin turned out to be fairly simple as well.  My rig to check the angle was a very last minute idea. :-) The rest is just deburring and dimpling and countersinking...  routine at this point.
