# Now a little of both elevators

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 6
Manual Ref: Page 9-12 and 9-13
Partner: N/A</span></em>

It was a busy weekend, but I managed to spend a few hours on the elevators.  The plans made it seem like the pulled rivets towards the rear spar (small area in the back of the 2nd and third pictures) would be more challenging than they actually were. Having the special bucking bar for the rear spar made that part very easy.  The rest was just a lot of squeezing.
