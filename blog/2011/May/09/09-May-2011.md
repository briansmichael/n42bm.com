# Cutting the weights and ribs

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 2
Manual Ref: Page 9-15 step 8 and 9-16 step 2
Partner: N/A</span></em>

The lead weights that come with the kit are quite interesting to cut with a band saw.  I've never worked with actual lead before.  It truly is a "soft" metal.  I think I spent as much time cleaning the blade of the band saw as I did cutting the weights.  The ribs for the trim tabs went much quicker.
