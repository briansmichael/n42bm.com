I had a few hours that I could devote to deburring, so I made a bit of progress.  The right tailcone skin is now complete and ready for priming.  All that is really left is all those small gaps between the flanges of the various bulkheads, the left tailcone skin, half of the bottom tailcone skin, and the back top skin.  At the rate I'm going, that should all take through the weekend.
