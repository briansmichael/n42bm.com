# Even more HS riveting

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 9
Manual Ref: Page 8-13 step 2, 3, and 4
Partner: N/A</span></em>

In my usual way, I took my time bucking the rivets along the front spar of the horizontal stabilizer.  I think I'm actually getting used to riveting with the rivet gun.  The only problem is... there are a LOT of rivets!  And to think, I'm not even on the wings yet!  There's still more to do, so maybe I'll finish tomorrow afternoon... maybe.  Oh, and I made another <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/04_23_2011/Horizontal_Stab_Riveting.mov">time-lapse video</a> for everyone's enjoyment :)
