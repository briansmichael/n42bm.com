I thought I'd try dimpling the rib flanges after I primed them this time.  It seems to work pretty well.  The process of applying the acetone I've been using (a paper towel) goes a whole lot better, as the dimpled holes tend to tear up the paper towel faster.  The dimpling doesn't seem to affect the primer too much when done after the fact.
