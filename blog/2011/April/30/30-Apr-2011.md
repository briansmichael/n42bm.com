# Priming elevator parts

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 7
Manual Ref: Page 9-10 step 1
Partner: N/A</span></em>

The time has come yet again to break out the acetone, alumiprep, and primer.  Thankfully, I had already primed a bunch of the elevator parts along with the HS.  All that remained were the rear spars, skins, and some of the ribs.  After all the priming was finished, it was time for my usual final test fit.  Everything appears to be in order.  Looking forward to riveting some more :)
