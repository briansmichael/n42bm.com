# More HS riveting

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5
Manual Ref: Page 8-13 step 1
Partner: N/A</span></em>

I've ordered a longeron yoke for my squeezer so that I can get at those hard to reach rivets in the forward HS spar.  It won't arrive 'till Fri, so in the mean time I decided to rivet the nose ribs to the skins.  I seem to have misplaced my soldering iron, so I'm trying a new technique of removing the vinyl from the skins... a pocket knife.  So far, I have far fewer scratches, so I think I may keep with this technique.  Albeit, it is a LOT slower.  Hopefully I'll be making a lot of progress this weekend :-)
