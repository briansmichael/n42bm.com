# Providence Canyon

For our 2nd annual hike, Roger, Carrie, and I took a trip to south Georgia to see Providence Canyon.  The Georgia State Parks website states that the canyon was started due to "poor agricultural processes".  I'm sure humans had a hand in starting all of it, but mother nature definitely could have done it all on her own.  The soil is quite loose, but not quite sand, so it washes away with water quite readily.  Regardless, it is neat to see Georgia's "Grand Canyon".  The hiking is pretty easy, too.  Don't let the ranger description of "difficult" fool you.  This is an easy hike (for the experienced hiker).  The elevation change is not more than a couple hundred feet, and most of it is flat.  For this trip, we even brought along Carrie's 5 year old son, who had no trouble handling the 10 or so miles of trails.
