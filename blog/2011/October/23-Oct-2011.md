# Left fuel tank

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 6
Manual Ref: Page 18-5 steps 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11
Partner: Bill Thomason</span></em>

After having helped Bill with his tanks for most of the afternoon, he came over to help me to get start on the left fuel tank.  It didn't take but a couple hours to get everything in place with sealant, but we only riveted the stiffeners.  I started working on filling in the holes (leading edge areas and shop-head side of any driven rivets) after he left.  I still have to drive rivets in the flanges of the ribs and the J-channel stiffener.

Being tired of applying sealant, and being too late to drive rivets, I went back to pre-assembling the wing... at least until I just had to get some rest for the night.
