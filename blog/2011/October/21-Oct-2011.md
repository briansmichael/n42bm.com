# Cutting holes in the left leading edge

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 2

Time to work on the left leading edge.  This time there are a couple more holes to open up.  The landing light hole took a little longer with the nibbler, but I felt better not shaking the entire leading edge with a jig saw.  The access cover for the stall warning system went easy enough.  The doubler made the whole process fairly straight forward.  I think the only hole left to do is for the pitot.  I may hold off on that one for a while.
