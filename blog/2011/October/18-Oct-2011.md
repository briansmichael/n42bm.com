# Rear spar match drilling

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 2
Manual Ref: Page 15-3 steps 1 through 4
Partner: n/a</span></em>

Match drilling the rear spar doublers was a pretty simple task.  The hardest part is copying the aileron pushrod hole in the spar to the doubler.  Also, the hole as shaped in the spar is different than as shown in the plans.  Since the plans seem to be a rough estimate anyway, I'm sticking to the shape on the spar itself.
