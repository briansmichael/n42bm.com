# Tailcone aft bulkhead riveting

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 8
Manual Ref: Page 10-20 Steps 6 &amp; 7; Page 10-21 Steps 3 &amp; 4
Partner: N/A</span></em>

In an effort to finish up the tailcone, I spent a few hours on the 1011 and 1012 bulkheads and on any rivets that needed to be drilled out and re-set (there were a few, but not too many of those).  I still have about 20 or so rivets left on the bottom surface to do.  Those are a bit tricky, as they are on a curved surface.  Since I mostly work alone, those are going to take a little more effort.  I did, however, get a few of the rivets along the longhorns set, and the <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/rv10/2011/07_28_2011/VOR_antenna_doubler_riveting.mov">doubler</a> for the VOR antenna finished up.  It sure is nice seeing it all together without the clecos in place! :-)
