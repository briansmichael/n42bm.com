# Static ports

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 2
Manual Ref: N/A
Partner: N/A</span></em>

I ordered the Cleaveland static ports, just to have something a little better than the pop rivets supplied in Van's static kit.  It only took a couple hours to resize the holes in the tailcone skins, match drill the holes, machine countersink the static ports, and get it all riveted.
