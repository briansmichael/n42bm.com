# Georgia Air Challenge

For the <a href="http://www.georgiaairchallenge.com/">Georgia Air Challenge</a>, sponsored by the Atlanta Aero Club, ATP and others, I took <a href="http://www.theflightschool.com/">The Flight School</a>'s Zodiac up for a circle route around Atlanta.  My plan was as follows:

KLZU -&gt; KRYY	2gal	32nm		00:22 ETE
KRYY -&gt; KCTJ	2gal	36nm		00:24 ETE
KCTJ -&gt; KFFC	2gal	33nm		00:22 ETE	Stopping to pick up some items at AircraftSpruce
KFFC -&gt; KMLJ	4gal	68nm		00:45 ETE
KMLJ -&gt; KLZU	3gal	61nm		00:41 ETE
---------------------------------------------------
Total			13gal	230nm	2:35 ETE in-flight

Those times were all "in-flight" times, so I expected the trip to take a little longer than that.  The sky over Atlanta was fairly clear, so I managed to get a few pictures.

My first stop was Cobb County's <a href="http://mccollumairport.com/">McCollum</a> field (KRYY).  I've been to KRYY before, but since it is more than 25nm from LZU, it counts in the challenge.  The landing was un-eventful, but while on the ground, I saw a friend's L-39 taxi'ing to runway 28.  I got a few pictures, and a <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/events/GeorgiaAirChallenge_07_23_2011/IMG_2150.mov">short video</a> of the takeoff.

After KRYY, I was off to West Georgia Regional - Carrollton (a.k.a. KCTJ).  The weather was degrading quite a bit, so before I could make it to the field, I chose to divert to my next planned stop, Falcon Field in Peachtree City, GA (a.k.a. KFFC).  While there, I took advantage of being close to <a href="http://www.aircraftspruce.com">Aircraft Spruce</a>, and called up the shuttle to visit the store.

<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/events/GeorgiaAirChallenge_07_23_2011/IMG_2151.jpg"><img style="margin: 5px;" src="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/events/GeorgiaAirChallenge_07_23_2011/IMG_2151_SM.jpg" alt="" width="200" height="149" /></a>

After KFFC, I don't have any pictures, but the rest of the trip went fairly well.  The landing at Milledgeville was a first for me.  I'd never landed with an approach over water before.  Granted, it was just a lake, but it was still a water approach.  Very neat.

Here are some screenshots from my iPhone of the trip route and flight plan.  In the end, I have 3 takeoffs and landings at airports in Georgia, more than 25nm from KLZU, during the month of July and the trip took 3.2 hours.
