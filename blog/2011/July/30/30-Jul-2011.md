# Tubing in Helen, GA

This year's trip to go tubing in Helen, GA was a little more eventful for me this than it has been in past years.  Since we had a few more people going, I needed to drive as well as my dad.  Which meant that I had to carry the car keys with me down the river...  needless to say, I dropped the keys somewhere along the way.  Thankfully, we had a spare...  but it was back in Stone Mountain.  In the end, everything has worked out ok.  I have a few loose ends to tie up, but all is well.
