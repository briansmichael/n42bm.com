# Main spar extension and nutplates

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 10
Manual Ref: Page 13-2 steps 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, and 8; 13-3 steps 1, 2, and 4
Partner: N/A</span></em>

Yet again, my "love" of priming has struck.  I've decided to move on to the wings, so I can get a few more parts ready for priming before I prime the doubler and battery stand parts. I do like the gold color of the alodine, I feel it is appropriate for the task of the main spar.  ;)  As for the build process, all I can say is that 10 hours of match and final drilling all those holes is VERY tiring!  The nutplates turned out extremely nice.  Still a few more to do (the other wing), but I'm happy with them, none-the-less.
