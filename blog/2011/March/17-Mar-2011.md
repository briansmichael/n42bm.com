# Tailcone top skins

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 6
Manual Ref: Pages 10-12 through 10-14
Partner: John Post</span></em>

I managed to knock out quite a bit today.  The top skins were much easier to work with since I had already taken them off and put them back on several times now.  The plans say to crimp the flanges with 2 holes.  At first, I didn't know if that would help any...  it does make the holes in the skins line up a little better with the bulkheads.  Also, the stiffeners were much easier to drill.  I guess it was because I was drilling down, rather than sideways.  The elevator stops and shoulder harness anchors went pretty quickly, as John did the drilling on those.
