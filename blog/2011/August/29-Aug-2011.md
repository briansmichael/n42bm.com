# Denali Day 9 - Kantishna

It was a very cold night. We later found out from a ranger that it got down to 28 degrees F overnight.

Our hike back to the road didn't take long, and we eventually got on a bus Eielson. Where we switched buses for the rest of the trip out to Kantishna.

Upon arrival we checked in at the skyline lodge, run by Katair. If you ever get a chance, stay with them and take a scenic air tour up to see Denali.  Katair had some seats available on a couple scenic flights around the mountain in the evening, and we agreed to go.  It was quite an experience. Our pilots were Andy and Connie, and both truly know their stuff.  Needless to say, I got a few pictures and videos (<a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Denali_2011/IMG_2525.MOV">4:17 min</a> <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Denali_2011/IMG_2545.MOV">2:45min</a> <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Denali_2011/IMG_2546.MOV">5:00 min</a> <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/trips/Denali_2011/IMG_2560.MOV">1:32 min</a>). Later in the evening, after Dave and the owner Greg, had a mini ping-pong match, we found out that the Aurora was visible the night before. So Dave and I stayed up until about 1 AM in an attempt to see it.  No joy :(
