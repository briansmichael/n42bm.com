# Denali Day 10 - The Return Home

With only a few hours sleep, we got up, had breakfast, and hopped in the plane for the trip back to the park entrance. This time all of us were in the plane with Connie. He was quite entertaining, and managed to get us some time around Denali on the way back.

Our car was literally on the other side of the railroad tracks from the landing strip, so we didn't have far to walk with our packs.  That was nice.  After a little repacking for the flight home from Anchorage, we got some lunch and started the 5 hour drive back to Anchorage.  On the drive, we tried to figure out what there was to do in Anchorage for the 8 or so hours that we would be there.  Turns out, there isn't all that much to do in Anchorage.  We did a little walking around town and got dinner at a local pizza cafe.

We all had the same flight from Anchorage to Phoenix. Then we split up, Dave and Jonathan heading to Boston, and I went to Atlanta.  All in all about 36 hours of travel time, with about 15 hours of actual "moving" time.
