# Denali Day 4 - Section 2

We continue bushwhacking up the mountain until we finally get up to the ridge.  Along the way we see a large "glacial erratic" on the ridge to the north, and evidence of local wildlife.  We hiked along the ridge for a while and setup camp towards the western side of section 2.
