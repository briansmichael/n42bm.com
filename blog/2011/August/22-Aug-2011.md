# Denali Day 2 - Arrival at the Park

After a few hours rest, we get some breakfast in the main lodge.  The view off the back porch is meant to be able to see Mt. McKinley, 40 miles away, according to the sign.  We weren't too lucky this morning.

After another 2 hour drive, we arrive at the Denali National Park entrance and make our way to the backcountry office to figure out what we can do.  We get an extra bear vault, buss passes, food for the hike, and stay overnight in Riley Creek campground.
