# Denali Day 7 - Toklat

As we were preparing for our bus ride to section 9, the camp host came over to tell us that Denali (a.k.a. Mt. McKinley) was very clearly visible.  And it was, but the snow was too much for my iPhone to handle, so it just looks like a took a picture of a road through some trees.

We hopped on a bus and ride the 3 hours to Toklat river.  On the way, we passed by the Teklanika river, which means large river, little water in the native Athabascan tongue of the area.   Shortly after passing the Teklanika, we came upon some sheep.  The bus driver put it best when she said "well, I guess we are now on sheep time".

After departing the bus, we started our hike south, up the Toklat.  The backcountry rules state that we must camp at least 1/2 mile and out of sight of the road.  That was quite a challenge as you can see nearly all of section 9 from the road.  We did eventually find a spot about 3.5 to 4 miles up the river
