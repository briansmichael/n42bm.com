# Denali Day 8 - Day of rest

Jonathan and Dave wanted to go see one of the glaciers towards the southern end of section 9. My feet were too worn out to make the 18 mile round trip, so I stayed in camp to rest.  When I woke up, there were 4 doll sheep resting on the southern face of divide mountain, just to the west of me.
