# Denali Day 3 - The hike begins

After a short planning session, we began our hike upstream on Riley Creek through section 1.  This was the only trail we traversed in the park.  The trail close to the Riley Creek campground is quite well maintained, complete with bridges and a nice 4 foot wide path.  After getting just south of little creek, we left the trail and started downhill to Riley Creek for our first crossing into section 2.  We also crossed little creek so as to stay on the ridge just north of it.  Bushwhacking up the ridge was rather challenging but we eventually found a place to camp.  Later we'd realize this was the best campsite we'd have the whole trip.
