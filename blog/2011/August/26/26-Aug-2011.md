# Denali Day 6 - Savage River

Bushwhacking was the keyword for the day's hike. They say not to follow the animal trails, but I don't know if it would have been possible not to.  The willow is really dense in this area.  We only had 1 river crossing, the Jenny, and it was pretty close to the savage river campground.  It took us so long to bushwhack our way down the river, we missed our bus to section 9.  The camp host at savage river was extremely nice and let us stay over night in their teepee.  Luxurious accomodations compared to what we'd been staying in.  The campground had a program called "tracks and skat" which we attended  I made sure to verify with the ranger giving the presentation that the stuff we'd been walking on was in fact tundra.
