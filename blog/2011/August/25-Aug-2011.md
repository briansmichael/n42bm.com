# Denali Day 5 - Section 4

While having breakfast we saw a mother grizzly bear and her 3 cubs descend from the ridge just above us.  They were just coming down to get some water and went pack up over the ridge.  We were definitely closer than the 1/4 mile the rangers say to stay away from bears.  Needless to say, we were a little shaken up. Throughout the day we would see more bears (8 total) and a moose and caribou. The first part of the day's hike was up along the ridge line, but when we crossed into section 4, it was pretty solid bushwhacking down to the savage river.  We setup camp just 100 yards off the river bank.
