# Denali Day 1 - The Flights Out

Atlanta (KATL) -&gt; Philadelphia (KPHL) -&gt; Anchorage (PANC) -&gt; McKinley Princess Lodge.

As is customary with travel in the U.S., I had a layover (about 3 hours) in Philadelphia, PA.  The terminal there is quite nice.
