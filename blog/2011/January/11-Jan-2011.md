I may have skipped a step or two, but I wanted to see how it all looks when it comes together.  Now that I have a few pictures, it is time to take it back apart and keep deburring.  There was a side benefit to putting it all together.  The plans say to make a 9 degree bend in a few pieces.  9 degrees is pretty hard to discern, so seeing the pieces in their final positions made it very easy to see if there was too much or too little bend.  All-in-all, I really don't have too much more to go on the horizontal stabilizer.
