# My First Helper

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5
Manual Ref: Various steps on Pages 9-8, 9-9, and 9-10
Partner: Chase Michael</span></em>

For a couple hours in the morning I managed to finish getting the spars and ribs of the elevators deburred.  Later in the day, my youngest nephew, Chase, wanted to check out how the plane was coming along.  While he was there, I had him help me out with a few steps.  Naturally, the order in the plans wasn't exactly followed, but he certainly enjoyed putting the pieces together.  In the picture where the right elevator is pictured with him between the workbenches, he managed to place all the clecos pictured.  Not bad for a 6 year old!
