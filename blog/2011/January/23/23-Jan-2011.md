# First Flight Field... Almost

The Thursday Night Gang made an attempt to visit First Flight Field (KFFA) where the Wright Brothers did their flying a little over 100 years ago.  Apparently it is easier to get there on the ground than by air.  We didn't quite make it to the field, but we got really, really close.  It was excellent IFR training experience, though.
