<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5.5

This was a lot of cleco pulling and moving, as well as drilling!  There truly are a lot of holes in the horizontal stabilizer.  This is also the first time I'm using the right-angle drill that I picked up from Harbor Freight.  It works pretty well, but I probably should have practiced a little more on scrap pieces than I did.  Thankfully, I didn't scratch the stringer web (HS-1016) too much.  After doing a little reading of other builder's logs, I think I want to spend a little more time deburring all these parts.  Just to be safe.  I don't want to end up with any cracks because I was going too fast and didn't properly debur something during the build process ;-)
