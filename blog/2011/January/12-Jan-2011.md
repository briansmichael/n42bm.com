# Backfilling steps

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 3.5
Manual Ref: Page 8-7, Steps 1 &amp; 3; Page 8-8, Steps 1 through 8

I spent a few hours taking apart the "test fit" I had done the day before and completed the deburring work on the HS-1004 and HS-905 ribs.  Then it was a simple matter of following the steps on page 8-8.  Mostly it was a lot of final drilling with the #30 drill bit and cleco'ing all the parts together.  I thought I had enough cleco's until I made my wings kit purchase, I was wrong.  Apparently the horizontal stabilizer uses a LOT of 3/32 clecos.  Needless to say, I have an order in for another 100 (along with a 3/32 cupped set for my squeezer :-) )
