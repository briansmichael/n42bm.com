# Priming tank "zees"

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5
Manual Ref: Page 18-4 steps 1 and 8
Partner: N/A</span></em>

The time had come to break out the HPLV gun yet again to do a little priming.  Thankfully, the tanks don't have much priming to be done (or at least should not be done).  After priming, the nut plates went on fairly smoothly.  Then it was back to deburring.  My debarring tool had apparently gotten a little less efficient, as I put some WD-40 on it and now I'm back to being able to debut with ease.  Something to remember, for sure!
