# Tailcone aft top skin riveting

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 5
Manual Ref: Page 10-22 steps 2 &amp; 3; Page 10-23 steps 1 through 5; Page 10-24 step 3
Partner: Bill Michael</span></em>

Getting back into airplane manufacturing mode, I had my dad come over to help me rivet the aft top skin onto the tailcone.  It took a couple hours, and l did have to drill out a few rivets, but all-in-all, it went fairly smoothly.  After a "brief" visit to LZU I was back in the garage to finish up a few steps that I could do without disturbing the neighbors too much.  I estimate about 25 more rivets, and I'll have completed the tailcone steps!
