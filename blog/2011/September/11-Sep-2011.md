Match drilling the fuel cap to the skin was easier than I thought it was going to be.  I'm still glad I took my time with it.  As for the plans, I only skipped over one part.  The plans have you attach the nut plates to the attach zees as a part of step 1.  I like to batch up a lot parts to be primed before actually priming anything.  I guess others don't have the same dislike I have for priming.
