# My First RV-10 Experience

I finally got a chance to see a completed version of what I've been building.  Not only did I get to see an RV-10, I actually got to got for a short flight in one!  I can safely say, I picked the right plane.

Thanks to Bill Thomasson, we took the short flight from KLZU (Gwinnett County) to GA04 (Mallards Landing) to pickup a bag Bill "forgot".  This was to be a day of firsts for me, as it was also my first time landing on a grass strip.  Naturally, Bill was PIC, but it was still an experience <a href="http://www.brianmichael.org/media/events/RV10_first_flight/IMG_1980.mov">landing</a> on grass.

Mallards Landing is such a nice place, I could definitely see myself living there (or somewhere like it) someday.  Everyone's got to have a dream, right?

Thankfully, Vic was very gratious and invited us over to see his RV, and took us up for a joy ride.  Since this was his 8th (I think) RV, he definitely knows a thing or two about building RVs, and was very helpful with advise on things to do and not do while building.  After a quick briefing on safety issues, we were in the plane.  Vic mentioned a quote that I like a lot, to show that "Experimental" is a very subjective term.  "The Ark was built by Noah, who was an amateur, the Titanic was built by professionals, and it crashed." :-)

As for handling, I don't think it will take me long to get used to it.  Flying the Zodiac helped a little, but the -10 is soooooo much faster!  Bill was a little less timid at the controls and managed to maintain altitude a bit better than me.  I just kept wanting to climb (not a good thing under the class B of Atlanta).  All in all, the weather was perfect, the plane was perfect, and the experience was something I'll remember for a long time.
