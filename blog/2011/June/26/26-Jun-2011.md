# Lunch in Greenville

In sortof a "sendoff" of Janet, Dave suggested we head up to Greenville Downtown airport (KGMU) for lunch.  I rented the Zodiac and Dave took his 172 Hawk XP.  Oddly, there were 4 of us going on the trip, but only me in the Zodiac and 3 in the 172.  How's that for a difference in carrying capacity!  The picture of me in the Zodiac is thanks to Dave.
