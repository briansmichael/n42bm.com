# Tailcone stiffener riveting

<em><span style="color: #000000;">Hours: 9
Manual Ref: Page 10-20 Steps 1 &amp; 2
Partner: N/A</span></em>

After a successful test fit of all the parts, it was time to start riveting again.  9 hours later, I was done with the stiffeners!  There are a LOT of rivets on the tailcone.  Most of the time was spent just rotating the tailcone around to position it for back riveting.  In the end, I only had a few rivets that need to be drilled out due to over squeezing.  Once I take care of those, I'll move on to step 3, riveting the bulkheads and bottom skins to side skins.
